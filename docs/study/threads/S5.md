**Do.**
1. A dot in the margin beside every markable passage and anchored list item; tap opens lost / easy / forgot / clear; one tap on a marked dot clears. Dots are buttons the Pencil can tap.
2. On a verb: `ctx.engine().triage(doc, anchor, verb)`, then act as the demo's reader does: `rebuild-basic` (log `rebuild-request` after a two-tap confirm; show "Rebuild requested"), `scaffold` (a sheet with the basics the passage rests on, their stages, links to them, and an attempt surface `ink.surface(doc, el, 'attempt:<anchor>')` whose changes log an `attempt` event, debounced 2 s), `eligible` ("Request rewrite (n of 3 left)", two taps, logs `rewrite-request`), `budget-exhausted` ("Drill <basic> instead", opens its first derive prompt), `requested`, `noted`. Always log the `mark` event.
3. Re-check open marks when events or content change (a rebuild landing clears the requested state and shows a toast with the ink counts from `PageInk.reanchor`).
4. Changes panel: build, budget, pending requests, marks, and the doc's `## Changes` section.

**Acceptance.** View test section (with a mock engine until S2 merges, then the real one with an injected clock): lost in a topic shows the scaffold; 24 h later with attempt ink it becomes eligible; two taps log a request and the budget drops; a lost mark in a basic logs a rebuild request; clear works; the Changes panel lists it all.
