**Goal.** The study view: open a study doc, render it passage by passage with anchors, follow links with a finger, write with the Pencil, go back, peek, switch tabs, and mount blocks, page ink and marks through the seams in `src/study/view/host.ts`.

**Files.** `src/study/view/*` except `marks.ts`, `changes.ts` and `host.ts`; `src/study/content/loader.ts` (extend if needed); the study section of `src/settings.ts` and its settings tab rows (root, device, minutes, devTools; see `StudySettings` in `context.ts`), stored under `settings.study`; a `/* study */` section at the end of `styles.css`; `test/run_study_test.py`; additions to `test/mock-obsidian.js`.

**Do.**
1. `ItemView` with view type `notebook-study`, registered from `src/study/view/register.ts`. Commands: "Open study" (`open-study`, the library), "Open as study doc" (`open-as-study`, also in the file menu of a study doc). Protocol handler `obsidian://notebook?study=<id>&a=<anchor>`. A ribbon icon for the library.
2. Library: study docs grouped by kind and area, with search over titles and anchored passage text (never proofs, solutions or bank tags).
3. Rendering per SPEC.md (The study view): 800 paper px scaled to the view width (max 1.4x), right margin 120 px; each passage rendered on its own with `MarkdownRenderer.render` into `div.sr-passage[data-a]`; anchored list items get `data-a`; passages with a `block` mount through `mountFor` in `src/study/blocks/index.ts` with a `BlockEnv`; the label as a small byline; solutions are never rendered as passages.
4. Input rules exactly as SPEC.md: Pencil writes and never follows links on the page; finger tap follows (under 10 px and 300 ms), finger hold 450 ms peeks, finger drag scrolls, fingers never draw. Reuse the notebook's stylus handling ideas (`src/ink/input.ts`, `blockStylusTouch`).
5. Back stack with scroll restore and a flash on the target; tabs (at most 6, active kept in view); header buttons Library, Back, Today, Map (call the panels' commands, `open-study-today` and `open-study-map`, which S8 registers), Changes (call `openChanges` from `view/changes.ts`, which S5 owns; a stub is fine until then).
6. Page ink through `createInkApi(ctx).page(...)` and marks through `attachMarks(host)` (stubs now; S4 and S5 fill them).
7. Re-render a doc when its file changes (`ctx.onChange('content')`), keeping scroll.

**Acceptance.** `test/run_study_test.py` (Playwright on `test/harness.html`, like `run_view_test.py`) loads the fixtures from `test/fixtures/study/` into the mock vault under `study/` and checks: the library lists them; a doc renders every passage with its `data-a`; a finger tap on a link opens the target and flashes it; Back restores the scroll; a 450 ms finger hold shows a peek; pen events over a link do not navigate; blocks mount (placeholders are fine); 1024x768 and 390x844 both work. Add the test to `npm test`.

**Notes.** `MarkdownRenderer.render` needs a `Component`: use the view. Extend `test/mock-obsidian.js` with a small `MarkdownRenderer.render` (a minimal markdown-to-HTML converter is enough for the tests) and `vault.adapter.append`.
