# S17: Circuit lab page

Brief by claude, 2026-11-18. Read docs/study/BOARD.md (how to work here), SPEC.md and FORMAT.md first.

**No release · claude · 8 h · starts now.**

**Goal.** Circuits practice with prediction and simulation on the iPad before the final, in Safari, with no release. The page is also the reference implementation for S24, as `lab/study-reader/` was for 0.15.

**Scope.**
1. **First commit, before anything else** (the scratchpad is temporary): the spike solver as `lab/circuit-lab/src/mna.mjs` and its 15 checks as `test/reference/check.mjs`, with a header saying the circuits are model-written.
2. **The page:** `lab/circuit-lab/`, one page that needs no build.
3. **Switching circuits** (at least six invented topologies), with fresh standard values every round:
   - Adit sketches $v(t)$ with the Pencil on symmetric axes scaled from the sources;
   - Run overlays the solver's curve and shows a table of $0^-$, $0^+$, $\infty$ and $\tau$, plus the sketch's errors;
   - ×½ and ×2 on each element ask for a new prediction first.
4. **AC:** phasors at one frequency (magnitudes, phases, a phasor diagram) and a log sweep (magnitude and phase) for RC, RL, RLC and an op-amp filter. If the MIE342 final does not cover AC, this part is skipped.
5. **Storage:** localStorage only, wrapped in try/catch.
6. **Publishing:** a private artifact.

**Out of scope.** Personal data; course problems; logging into the study system.

**Files owned.** `lab/circuit-lab/*`, `test/reference/*`.

**Acceptance.**
- `node test/reference/check.mjs`: 15 of 15 pass.
- 20 seeded value draws per topology solve without a singular matrix.
- The page works at 390 px and with a mouse in Chromium.
- **iPad:** Adit sketches and runs two circuits with the Pencil in Safari and says whether the scoring feels fair. That answer sets the sketch-scoring tolerances for 0.16.
